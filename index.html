Track

Every run, walk and ride. Down to the kilometre.

Start, go, finish. Kin draws your route, counts your steps all day, and tells you what the effort was worth.

  • Clean routes. GPS jitter is filtered out, so waiting at a crossing doesn’t pile up distance.
  • Best efforts. Your fastest 1K, 5K and 10K inside every activity, with a gold PR the day you beat one.
  • Splits, pace and climb. Every kilometre laid out, the quickest one lit.

Compete

Race your friends, not strangers.

A leaderboard means more when you know everyone on it. Kin ranks you against the people you actually train with.

  • Weekly challenges. Today, this week, this month: one board, the person to catch right at the top.
  • Segments. Mark the hill, the sprint, the loop by the lake. Every pass is timed, and friends race you for the top spot.
  • Head-to-head. Swipe through the last two weeks against any friend, one day at a time. Crown for the winner.

Share

Cards worth posting.

Every activity gets a card made to be shared: your route drawn clean, the distance big, the record in gold. Save it, post it to the Kin feed, or send it anywhere.

  • Month and year in review. Your totals, your best day, and every day of the period as a calendar that glows.
  • Badges that mean something. Struck medals from bronze to platinum, from a first 5K to a marathon.

Together

The run is better with company.

Kin is built around the few people who get you out of the door, not a follower count.

Run Together

Start a shared run with your crew. Everyone records on their own phone; Kin adds up the distance, shows who's out right now, and ranks the day.

Beacon

Heading out alone or after dark? Turn on Beacon and the people you send the link to can see where you are, until you finish.

Feed and kudos

Your friends' runs with their routes and records, photos and videos. Give kudos, leave a comment, keep each other going.

Groups

The running club, the office, the family. Each group gets its own board, so you know exactly which crew you're winning.

A partner who notices

Pick one friend as your accountability partner. It’s much harder to skip a run when someone will notice.

Messages

Plan tomorrow's route, trade photos, trash-talk the leaderboard. One chat per friend, right next to your stats.

Your data

Yours first.

Kin works fully without an account, and keeps your history on your phone. You decide what goes further.

  • No account needed. Steps, activities, routes and goals live on your device until you choose to sign in.
  • Share on purpose. A route only leaves your phone when you post that activity, create a segment, or switch on Beacon for a run.
  • Nothing sold. Your health, step and location data are never sold or handed to advertisers.
  • Gone when you say. Delete your account from inside the app, and everything on the server goes with it. Read the privacy policy.
